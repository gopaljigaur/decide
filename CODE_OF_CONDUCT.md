# Code of conduct

## The short version

Argue about the code, not the person. Assume the other side read the thing and
reached a different conclusion for a reason.

## What that means here

- Review the change, not the author. "This drops the reported confidence" is a
  review; "you clearly do not understand calibration" is not.
- Bring the evidence. A number, a repro, a line reference. A claim with none of
  those is an opinion, and it is fine to say so about your own.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and unwelcome attention of any kind are not
  tolerated, in issues, pull requests, or anywhere else this project is
  discussed.
- Nobody owes you a reply on your schedule. This is maintained by one person.

## Reporting

Message [@gopaljigaur](https://github.com/gopaljigaur) on GitHub. Reports stay
private. The maintainer will act on what the evidence supports, which may mean
editing or deleting a comment, closing a thread, or blocking an account.

Since one person maintains and enforces this, a report about that person has
nowhere internal to go. Take it to GitHub, which has its own
[reporting process](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
